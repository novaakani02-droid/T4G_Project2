Occupational therapy foundations

Built on Occupational Therapy

Occupational Therapy focuses on helping people participate in the activities they need, want, or are expected to do(occupations) AbleSpace brings this approach into everyday life through practical tools and resources.

1. Everyday Function

OT looks beyond a diagnosis to understand what a person wants and needs to do,from dressing and cooking to studying, working, and participating in the community.

2. Bridging the gaps

Support doesn't stop when a therapy session ends. AbleSpace provides practical resources and routine tools that users can explore between professional sessions.

3. Dignity and Independence

Being able to participate in everyday activities can build confidence, support autonomy, and reduce unnecessary dependence on others.
