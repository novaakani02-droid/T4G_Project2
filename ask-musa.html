Ask Musa

Musa offers general, educational guidance on everyday activities — dressing, safety, devices, and more. It complements your therapist; it doesn't replace them.

Musa does not diagnose conditions, conduct assessments, or prescribe treatment. It provides education, encourages independence, and points you toward professional support when needed.
