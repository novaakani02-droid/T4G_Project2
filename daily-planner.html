Plan Around Your Energy

Different activities require different amounts of energy. Planning demanding activities around your energy level can help you balance activity, rest, and participation throughout the day.

Low Energy
  • Reading
  • Listening to music
  • Light grooming
  • Rest
  • Simple hobbies
Medium Energy
  • Dressing
  • Studying
  • Simple meal preparation
  • Light household activities
High Energy
  • Cleaning
  • Shopping
  • Meal preparation
  • Longer work/study sessions

Save Your Energy

  • Plan important activities ahead.
  • Take appropriate rest breaks.
  • Avoid rushing.
  • Prioritize essential activities.
  • Break larger activities into smaller activities.
  • Alternate demanding activities with easier activities.
