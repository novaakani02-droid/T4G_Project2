Frequently asked questions

Is Musa a licensed occupational therapist?

No. Musa is an educational AI companion. It does not diagnose, assess, or prescribe treatment — always consult a licensed OT for clinical decisions.

Is AbleSpace free to use?

Yes, this student prototype is free to explore. A future version may introduce optional therapist-led features.

Can my caregiver use my account?

Caregivers are welcome to sign up with their own account and select "Caregiver / family member" during sign-up.
